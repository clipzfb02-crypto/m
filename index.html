Questions

Good to know before you visit

Can't find your answer? Call us on (312) 555-0142 or send us a message.

Is the price on the website the price I pay?

Yes. The listed price is the price of the car. On top of it you'll only pay tax, title and registration, which the state sets, and our documentation fee, which is printed on every buyer's order. There are no add-ons you didn't ask for.

Can my own mechanic inspect a car first?

Of course. Take it to a shop near us, or have a mobile inspector meet you on the lot. Tell us when you book and we'll have the car ready.

How does the 7-day money-back guarantee work?

If the car isn't right, bring it back within 7 days and 250 miles in the condition you bought it, and we'll refund the purchase price.

Do you take trade-ins that still have a loan?

Yes. We pay off your lender directly. If the car is worth more than you owe, the difference goes toward your purchase or straight to you.

Can I buy a car without coming in?

Yes. We can handle the paperwork by phone and email, and deliver the car to your driveway within 50 miles.

What do I need to bring to buy a car?

Your driver's license, proof of insurance, and a down payment if you're financing. If you're trading in, bring the title or your loan details and all the keys.
